How we work

Open by default, rigorous by design

Papers, code, datasets and evaluations are published openly unless privacy or safety requires otherwise. Every result should be reproducible by another lab, anywhere in the world.

Each program is co-led by a senior researcher and an Academy fellow, so knowledge moves from research straight to the people learning it.
