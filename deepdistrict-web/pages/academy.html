FAQ

Questions

Do I need to know programming?

For Ignite and Mission Control, no. For Orbit and Deep Space, basic Python is expected. We publish a free preparation course before each intake.

What language is teaching in?

Slovenian and English. Ignite runs in Slovenian; research tracks run mostly in English.

How much does it cost?

The pilot cohort is free for admitted fellows. Long-term funding will combine public, EU and partner support.

When does Cohort 01 start?

The plan is the first quarter of 2027. Pre-register on the Join page to be notified when applications open.
