FAQ

Questions, answered

Can't find what you need? Contact us

Do I need to know programming?

For Spark and Professional, no. For Scholar and Fellow, basic Python is expected. We publish a free preparation course before each intake.

Can I join from outside Slovenia?

Yes. Scholar and Professional can be completed remotely, and Fellow positions are open to international applicants.

What language is teaching in?

English and Slovenian. Spark runs mainly in Slovenian; research tracks run mainly in English.

How much does it cost?

The pilot cohort is free for admitted fellows. Long-term funding will combine public, EU and partner support.

When does Cohort 01 start?

The plan is the first quarter of 2027. Pre-register to be notified when applications open.
