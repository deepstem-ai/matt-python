Learner records (enrolled students)ข้อมูลผู้เรียน (ผู้ที่ลงทะเบียนเรียน)

When you enrol, we keep a learner record: name, contact details, school or grade, a parent or guardian for learners under 20, your course and teacher, lesson dates, hours, topics and progress notes, and payments. We use it to teach, schedule lessons, report progress to you (or your guardian) and handle fees — on the basis of our contract with you, our legal duties for accounting, and consent where needed. Records are kept in our back office (on our own computer, or later with a hosting provider acting only as our data processor). Only staff who teach or administer your course can see them, and learners and parents can sign in to see their own records. When we email you a document (for example a receipt or schedule), it is sent through our email provider, and they are kept for as long as you study with us plus the period required by Thai tax and accounting law. You can ask for a copy, a correction or deletion at any time. If you choose to receive notifications by LINE, we store your LINE user ID to send them through our LINE Official Account (LY Corporation); you can disconnect it at any time in the portal.
