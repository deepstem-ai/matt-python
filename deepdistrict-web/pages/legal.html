Privacy policy

DeepDistrict.si ("we") respects your privacy and processes personal data in line with the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and Slovenian data-protection law.

What we collect

  • Details you give us in forms: name, email, country, your interests and your message.
  • Your email address if you subscribe to updates.

Why we collect it

  • To answer your enquiry or process your application (legal basis: your consent and steps prior to entering an agreement).
  • To send updates you have asked for (legal basis: consent, which you can withdraw at any time).

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, delete or export your data, and you can object to or restrict processing. Contact privacy@deepdistrict.si. You may also lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner of the Republic of Slovenia.

Current prototype

In this prototype version, form entries are stored only in your own browser (local storage) and are not sent to us.

Terms of use

By using this website you agree to use it lawfully and not to disrupt it or misuse its content. Information on this site describes plans and concepts and may change without notice; it does not constitute an offer or a binding commitment. Links to external sites are provided for convenience and we are not responsible for their content.

Cookie policy

This website does not use tracking or advertising cookies. The browser's local storage is used only to remember form entries and newsletter sign-ups on your own device. If analytics are added in the future, we will ask for your consent first.

Accessibility statement

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at level AA. The site supports keyboard navigation, screen readers, visible focus states and reduced-motion preferences. If you find a barrier, please tell us at hello@deepdistrict.si and we will fix it.
