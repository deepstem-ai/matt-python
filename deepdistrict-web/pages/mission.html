Mission

Super Intelligence, built for people

We want SI to be researched, taught and built openly and safely, in a way people everywhere can trust and benefit from.

The concept

Beyond AI

Today's AI is very good at patterns: recognising, predicting and generating. SI (Super Intelligence) is the next step: systems that reason across domains, form and test their own hypotheses, and work alongside people to discover what no one knew before.

DeepDistrict treats SI as a long-term research goal and organises a whole district around it: research, education, innovation and governance.

SI Super Intelligence
Intelligence that goes beyond today's AI. It reasons, discovers and creates across every domain, while staying aligned with human values and under human oversight.

Principles

How we build responsibly

Six commitments from our founding charter. They apply to every program, fellow and venture in the district.

01

Human oversight

People stay in charge of goals, decisions and deployment. Safety research comes before scale.

02

Open science

Results, code and evaluations are public by default so that others can check and build on them.

03

Privacy by design

GDPR-grade data protection and alignment with the EU AI Act, built in from the start.

04

Talent everywhere

Every region gets a node, and remote tracks open the Academy to the world.

05

Sustainable compute

Efficient models and renewable-powered infrastructure, with energy use measured and published.

06

Public benefit

Health, climate, education and public services come first in our research priorities.
