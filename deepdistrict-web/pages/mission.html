Mission

Super Intelligence, built for people

Our mission is to make our region a place where Super Intelligence is researched, taught and built openly and safely, for everyone's benefit.

The concept

Beyond AI

Today's AI is very good at patterns: recognising, predicting and generating. SI, Super Intelligence, is the next step. These are systems that reason across domains, form and test their own hypotheses, and work with people to discover what no one knew before.

DeepDistrict treats SI as a long-term research goal and organises a whole district around it: research, education, innovation and governance.

SI /ˌes ˈaɪ/ · Super Intelligence
Intelligence that goes beyond today's AI: it reasons, discovers and creates across every domain, and stays aligned with human values and under human control.

Principles

How we build

P1

Human control

People stay in charge of goals, decisions and deployment. Safety research runs before scale.

P2

Open science

Results, code and evaluations are public by default so that others can check and build on them.

P3

Talent at home

Educate and keep talent in the region and give every region a node, not only the capital.

P4

Sustainable compute

Efficient models and renewable-powered infrastructure. Energy use is measured and published.

P5

Public benefit

Health, climate, education and public services come first in our research priorities.

P6

European values

Privacy, fairness and the rule of law, in line with EU regulation, by design.
